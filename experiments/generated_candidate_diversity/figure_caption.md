**Sequence and structural diversity across peptide selection and guided generation.**
**a**, Pairwise sequence percent identity (PID) among
the 24 synthesized peptide candidates. From left to right, the groups are linear and
cyclic candidates generated for *P. aeruginosa* PA5257 (12 and three peptides; 66 and
three pairs) and linear and cyclic candidates generated for *E. coli* AR-0349 (six
and three peptides; 15 and three pairs). Lin. and Cyc. denote linear and cyclic
peptides, respectively. Dots represent
individual pairs, thick vertical
bars show the interquartile range and white circles mark the median; violin densities
are omitted for the cyclic groups because each contains only three pairs. **b**,
Morgan-fingerprint Tanimoto-similarity distribution for the 73 peptide candidates
used for candidate-level review (71 distinct canonical isomeric structures). **c**,
Morgan-fingerprint Tanimoto-similarity distribution for the 84,226 MIC-guided outputs
generated across the two target strains and all generated lengths (83,433 distinct
canonical isomeric structures). Tanimoto similarities were calculated using
2,048-bit radius-2 Morgan fingerprints with chirality enabled.
